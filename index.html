Gobbler Games 2026 - The Fortune

Find your PIN

You'll need a 4-digit PIN to log in. Tap your name. Everyone's PIN is different, and nobody else can work out yours.

How The Fortune works

It runs all week on your phone, alongside the games on the agenda. Here is the short version.

  1. One of us secretly holds the Fortune. The app picks them at random. Everyone else is a hunter trying to work out who it is.
  2. By day, play the game on the agenda, and pull off a secret task if you are offered one.
  3. Each evening, everyone votes in secret for who they think holds the Fortune.
  4. The holder quietly banks points every night they are not caught. If they are caught, the Fortune moves to someone else, and nobody is told who.
  5. Most points at the end of the week wins the Gobbler Games and goes on the trophy.
Before the game starts
  • Log in with your name and your PIN. Jason and Emma share one login and play as a team.
  • Answer the short list of questions (favorite candy, dream vacation and so on). Answer honestly and keep your answers to yourself. Clues about the Fortune holder are built from them.
  • When everyone has answered, the host starts the game and the Fortune goes to a random player. Only that player's phone says so.
How it fits the agenda

The games on the agenda are the same games as always, and the Trophy Rules still apply: 2 points for first, 1 for second, ties share. The results now go into the app, and they do more than score.

  • Game 1: Build a tower (Sunday).
  • Game 2: Apples to Apples (Monday, 4:00).
  • Game 3: Basketball in the garage (Tuesday, 4:00).
  • Game 4: Wooden throwing game (Wednesday, 1:00).
  • Game 5: Bocce (Thursday, 4:00).
  • Game 6: Charades (Friday, 4:00).

After each game, everyone who placed picks a card in the app, first place first. Each first-place finisher also gets a private clue about whoever holds the Fortune right then.

Some secret tasks are tied to that day's agenda too, so keep an eye on anyone acting oddly at dinner.

The agenda crowns the winner on Saturday the 28th. That is when the last vote and the finale happen.

A day in the game
  1. Morning: the board shows last night's results. A few players are offered a secret task.
  2. Afternoon: the agenda game. Placing earns points, a card, and maybe a clue.
  3. Any time: play cards, ask the Keeper a question, start a rumour.
  4. Evening: the host opens the vote. You can change your vote until the host closes it.
  5. After the vote: the board updates once, all together, and you are told only whether the Fortune moved.

Points earned during the day stay hidden until that nightly update, so you cannot watch the board to see who just did something.

The night vote
  • Vote for the player you think holds the Fortune. You cannot vote for yourself.
  • Votes are secret. Nobody sees how anyone voted until the game is over.
  • A correct vote scores +1.
  • The holder votes too, but their vote is only a decoy and never counts.
  • The holder is caught only if they get more votes than any other player. A tie is not enough.
  • When the holder is caught, the Fortune moves to one of the players who voted correctly, picked at random. So voting right can make you the next holder.
  • The app announces only whether the Fortune moved. It never says who had it or who has it now.
If you hold the Fortune

Your job is to not get caught. Every night you survive, you bank secret points:

  • +2 for surviving the night.
  • +1 for each other player who drew at least one vote, up to +2. Spreading suspicion pays.
  • A streak bonus: +1 for your first night, +2 for your second, +3 for every night after.

That is up to +7 a night. Banked points stay hidden from the board until the Fortune leaves you or the game ends, so a quiet score does not mean a quiet week.

If the heat is on, you can ditch the Fortune during the day. It goes to a random other player. You cannot ditch on the final day.

Secret tasks
  • Each day the holder and a couple of other players are offered a secret task. You only see what it is after you accept.
  • Finish it before the vote closes and report it in the app: +2 and a random card.
  • Accept it and fail, or forget to report it: -2. Declining costs nothing.
  • Tasks are harmless things to do around the house, and never involve touching or moving other people's things.
  • Spotting: once a day you can name a player and describe the task you think they had. If you are right, +1. It is judged when the vote closes.
  • Do it quietly: if 3 or more players correctly spot your task, it pays only +1 instead of +2. You keep the card.
Clues
  • Clues come from the questions everyone answered before the game.
  • A clue might give the first letter or the length of the holder's answer to a question, an answer the holder did not give, or (rarely) the holder's full answer.
  • A clue was true on the day you got it. If the Fortune has moved since, it describes the old holder.
  • Clues are private. Share them, trade them or lie about them as you like.
Cards

You get cards by placing in an agenda game or finishing a secret task. Your hand is private. Most cards are played in secret.

  • Cancel: name a player. Their vote tonight is thrown out.
  • Gag: name a player. They lose their vote tonight. Everyone is told who was gagged, but not by whom.
  • Immunity: every vote cast on you tonight is erased.
  • Scatter: one vote cast on you tonight is moved onto a random player who got none.
  • Bounty: name a player. You score +1 for every vote cast on them tonight.
  • Tag Team: name a partner. If you both vote for the same person tonight, you score +2. If you are the only two who did, you also get a card.
  • Heir: if the holder is caught tonight, the Fortune comes to you.
  • Silence: tonight the app will not say whether the Fortune moved.
  • Points Swap: move 1 point from one player to another. Neither can be you.
  • Pickpocket: steal one random card from a player. They are not told.
  • Delegate: name a player. They are offered a secret task today.
  • Double Down: play it when you accept a secret task, before you see it. The task pays +4 or costs 4.
  • Challenge Advantage: a head start or extra try in the next agenda game. Everyone is told.
  • Handicap: name a player. They get a disadvantage in the next agenda game. Everyone is told, but not who did it.
The Keeper

The Keeper guards the vault and hosts the game inside the app. It posts a bulletin after each night's vote and sends everyone a private line.

  • You can ask it 3 questions a day on the Ask tab. It knows every rule. Your questions and its answers are private.
  • It does not know who holds the Fortune, so do not bother asking.
  • You can start 1 anonymous rumour a day. The Keeper rewords it and posts it to everyone with no name attached. True or false is up to you.
The finale
  • On the final day nobody can ditch the Fortune, and that night's vote is the last.
  • Then everyone gets a sealed case. The Fortune is in the case of whoever holds it.
  • The top two on the board are the finalists. The leader gets two fresh clues and second place gets one.
  • Second place moves first, then the leader. Each may secretly swap any two players' cases, or leave them all alone.
  • The cases open. Whoever ends up with the Fortune case scores a big bonus. It is always worth enough for second place to overtake the leader.
  • All the banked points are revealed and the highest total wins.

After that the vault is unsealed and everyone can read the full log: every vote, every task, every card, and who started each rumour.
